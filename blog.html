Practical tips

A little product knowledge goes a long way.

Simple reminders to help you choose and maintain common hardware and plumbing products.

01 BUYING GUIDE

Choosing plumbing pipes

Check the pipe’s intended use, diameter, pressure rating and compatibility with fittings. If you’re unsure, ask a qualified plumber before buying.

02 HOME CARE

Looking after water tanks

Choose a suitable capacity for your household and follow the manufacturer’s cleaning and maintenance instructions. Keep the lid securely closed.

03 BEFORE YOU BUY

Measure before buying fittings

Take note of the required size, connection type and where the item will be installed. A photo of the existing fitting can help when asking for a replacement.

These are general tips, not technical specifications. Always follow product instructions and consult a qualified professional when needed.
