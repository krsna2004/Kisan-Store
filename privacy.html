Privacy

Your information, handled with care.

This notice explains how Kisan Hardware & Sanitary Store uses information submitted through the customer enquiry form.

What we collect

If you submit the enquiry form, we collect your name, mobile number, locality, product enquiry, and your choices about contact and promotional updates.

How we use it

We use enquiry details to respond to your product request. If you separately tick the optional promotional consent box, we may also contact you with occasional store offers and updates by phone or WhatsApp.

Promotional messages are optional

You can submit a product enquiry without agreeing to promotional updates. To stop promotional messages, contact the store and ask us to stop.

Storage and access

Submitted details are stored in a Google Sheet managed by the store. Access to that sheet should be restricted to people who need it to handle enquiries.
