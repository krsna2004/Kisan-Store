Laal Ghoda Diesel Engine Oil 20W-40

Available

Laal Ghoda Diesel Engine Oil 20W-40 is a quality multigrade engine oil developed for the lubrication and protection of compatible diesel and petrol engines. Its 20W-40 viscosity grade helps maintain proper lubrication across different operating temperatures, supporting smooth engine operation.

This engine oil helps reduce friction between moving engine components, protects against engine wear and rust, and contributes to improved engine performance. It also helps maintain engine cleanliness and supports longer oil change intervals when used according to the vehicle manufacturer's recommendations.

Whether used in compatible diesel or petrol engine applications, Laal Ghoda 20W-40 Engine Oil is a practical choice for routine engine maintenance and reliable lubrication.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Multigrade Formulation: Designed to provide lubrication under varying operating temperatures.
  • Engine Wear Protection: Helps protect important engine components from wear and friction.
  • Rust Protection: Helps protect engine parts against rust and corrosion.
  • Improved Performance: Supports smooth and efficient engine operation.
  • Longer Oil Change Interval: Helps support extended oil service intervals as recommended by the manufacturer.
  • Versatile Application: Suitable for compatible diesel and petrol engines.
  • Convenient Packaging: Available in an easy-to-handle 1-litre bottle.

Applications

  • Compatible diesel engines.
  • Compatible petrol engines.
  • Vehicles and equipment requiring SAE 20W-40 engine oil with the appropriate API specification.
  • Routine engine lubrication and maintenance.
